## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an architectural, avant-garde digital presence for contemporary specialty coffee roasters, atelier tasting rooms, and high-end hospitality venues. Departing decisively from nostalgic, heritage earth tones, the aesthetic draws upon brutalist structural discipline, cool industrial mineral tones, and the sudden, precise ignition of high-voltage citrus-amber.

### Aesthetic Foundation
- **Brutalist Architectural Precision:** Strict right angles, structural perimeter boundaries, and monolithic plane divisions define spatial hierarchy.
- **Atmospheric Luminosity:** Surfaces combine cool stone-slate foundations with technical hairline segmentations and subtle frosted-glass overlays for floating interactive metadata.
- **High-Voltage Ignition:** The primary amber-orange acts as an energetic spark across pristine, cool titanium surfaces, evoking extraction temperature, technical mastery, and modern culinary energy.

### Target Audience & Voice
Engineered for coffee professionals, culinary tastemakers, and discerning patrons who view hospitality through the lens of modern craft, design discipline, and technical execution. The interface feels exacting, pristine, and uncompromising.

## Colors

The palette balances cool, laboratory-grade stone and titanium neutrals against the singular kinetic force of high-voltage orange.

### Palette Mechanics
- **Primary (`#FF5500`):** Kinetic amber-orange. Reserved strictly for primary functional anchors, interactive confirmations, active tasting notes, and critical alerts. Never applied as ambient backgrounds across large surfaces.
- **Secondary (`#2B2D42`):** Deep graphite slate. Serves as the authoritative structural counterweight, framing modular zones and grounding interactive components.
- **Tertiary (`#8D99AE`):** Cool anodized titanium. Governs architectural hairline dividers, inactive borders, secondary metrics, and technical metadata.
- **Neutral (`#1E2022`):** Deep iron carbon. Utilized for high-contrast headline typographic ink, deep structural slabs, and precision indicators.
- **Canvas (`#F4F5F7`):** Cool limestone white. A luminous, anti-glare architectural base that preserves clarity under bright cafe or mobile environments.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy juxtaposes the engineered, geometric tension of **Space Grotesk** against the neutral, ergonomic legibility of **Plus Jakarta Sans**.

### Font Roles
- **Display & Headlines (`Space Grotesk`):** Technical, structural, and assertive. Used in all headings, section demarcations, numerical cupping scores, and product titles.
- **Body & Longform (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Clean, welcoming, and optically balanced. Ensures extended tasting descriptions, origin journals, and operational instructions remain effortless to read.
- **Labels & Micro-Data (`Space Grotesk`):** Set with high tracking and uppercase styling (`label-sm`, `label-md`) to emulate physical scientific labeling, batch identifiers, processing methods, and temperature metrics.

## Layout & Spacing

The structural layout uses a modular, continuous-edge grid system inspired by architectural floor plans.

### Grid Framework
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column fluid grid, `margin`: 3rem (48px), `gutter`: 1.5rem (24px). Panels align along strict horizontal and vertical axes, separated by 1px hairline borders rather than arbitrary white gaps.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout, `margin`: 2rem (32px), `gutter`: 1rem (16px). Secondary information collates into horizontally scrollable carousels with visible cut-off markers.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column layout, `margin-sm`: 1.25rem (20px), `gutter-sm`: 1rem (16px). Elements span the full viewport width within containment cards.

### Spacing Discipline
Components leverage internal padding scales (`space-xs` through `space-xl`) strictly adhering to multiples of 4px and 8px. Negative space is functional, preventing visual congestion while preserving the clinical precision of the product catalogs.

## Elevation & Depth

To reinforce the brutalist architectural thesis, this system rejects conventional ambient, drop shadows in favor of **structural containment borders**, **tonal stone layering**, and **frosted technical glass**.

### Elevation Strategies
1. **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** `#F4F5F7` limestone surface. Flat, matte, non-reflective.
2. **Level 1 (Sub-Panels & Structural Modules):** Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) planes delineated by crisp 1px borders in `#8D99AE` at 40% opacity (`rgba(141, 153, 174, 0.4)`).
3. **Level 2 (Interactive Floating Trays & Modals):** Translucent frosted glass layers utilizing `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` with an 85% opacity `#FFFFFF` fill and a crisp `#2B2D42` outline (1.5px).
4. **Active Focus & High-Energy Depth:** Depth is communicated horizontally or via edge ignition. When an element is focused, hovered, or selected, it receives a stark `#FF5500` 1.5px outline or an instantaneous hard offset block-shadow: `3px 3px 0px #1E2022`.

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary is strictly non-rounded (`roundedness: 0`). Every corner, surface, input container, modal edge, and image frame concludes at a sharp 90-degree intersection.

### Geometric Rules
- **No Radii:** All corners are `0px`. Rounding introduces an organic softness that contradicts the industrial, architectural intent of this design system.
- **Edge Definition:** Visual interest is generated through chamfers, technical hairline crosses at container junctions, and hard boundary contrasts rather than smooth curves.
- **Circular Exception:** True 1:1 circular geometries are permitted only for technical operational states (e.g., cupping radar nodes, sensory color swatches, or status micro-pills).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** Solid `#FF5500` fill, pure white `#FFFFFF` text (`Space Grotesk` uppercase, `label-md`), 0px radius, zero drop shadow. On hover: shifts background to `#1E2022` with a 1px inner border of `#FF5500`.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Transparent background, 1.5px solid `#2B2D42` border, `#2B2D42` text. Hover state inverts to `#2B2D42` fill with `#F4F5F7` text.
- **Destructive / Caution:** `#1E2022` fill with a high-contrast `#FF5500` indicator line across the top edge.

### Chips & Badges
- **Cupping / Origin Tags:** Monolithic, sharp-edged chips with a 1px border of `#8D99AE` at 50% opacity, `#1E2022` text, and a micro-square (4px x 4px) indicator colored in `#FF5500` preceding the copy.
- **Process Badges (e.g., "ANAEROBIC NATURAL"):** Compact uppercase typography (`label-sm`), background in `#2B2D42`, text in `#FFFFFF`.

### Cards (Roast & Equipment Modules)
- **Structure:** White background, 1px solid border in `#8D99AE` (30% opacity). Images maintain 100% rectilinear aspect ratios with no corner softening.
- **Card Header:** Features origin and elevation details positioned alongside cupping score badges rendered in high-contrast `display-xl` sizing.
- **Hover Micro-Interaction:** Immediate hairline border transition from `#8D99AE` to `#FF5500`, accompanied by a 2px hard shift up-and-left (`transform: translate(-2px, -2px)` with `box-shadow: 2px 2px 0px #1E2022`).

### Input Fields & Selectors
- **Input Containers:** 0px radius, 1.5px perimeter line in `#8D99AE` (50% opacity), canvas background `#FFFFFF`. Typography in `Plus Jakarta Sans`.
- **Focus State:** Border switches instantaneously to `#FF5500` with an accompanying subtle technical label positioned directly on the top border line.

### Lists & Inventory Tables
- **Grid Layout:** Modular list rows separated by 1px solid horizontal borders in `#8D99AE` (25% opacity).
- **Data Alignment:** Variable cupping profiles, batch dates, and pricing aligned on the Space Grotesk tabular numeral baseline. Hovering a row triggers a subtle `#F4F5F7` tone highlight with an orange `#FF5500` 3px accent bar on the extreme left margin.

### Checkboxes & Radio Elements
- **Checkboxes:** Sharp-edged squares (18px x 18px), 1.5px border `#2B2D42`. Active state fills `#FF5500` with a stark white geometric checkmark.
- **Radio Buttons:** Sharp concentric squares (18px outer square, 8px solid `#FF5500` center block upon selection).

### Sensory Flavor Matrix
- Dedicated interactive chart mapping acidity, body, sweetness, and processing data. Built with technical crosshairs, high-contrast monochrome axes, and vivid `#FF5500` plot lines with zero bezier softening.
